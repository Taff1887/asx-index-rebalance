S&P/ASX Rebalance Announcements

All S&P/ASX indices are regularly rebalanced to ensure they remain liquid and tradable.

Index Rebalance Months
S&P/ASX 20 March, June, September and December.
S&P/ASX 50 March, June, September and December.
S&P/ASX 100 March, June, September and December.
S&P/ASX 200 March, June, September and December.
S&P/ASX 300 March and September.
All Ordinaries March and September.

When do rebalances occur?
• After the market closes on the third Friday of March, June, September and December.

How much notice is given?
• The market is notified two weeks prior to all upcoming constituent changes (starting Sept 2021)

What criteria are used for company selection?
• Additional details on the index calculation and company selection methodology can be found on the index methodology page.

As the S&P/ASX 200 is Australia's flagship index, additional announcements (e.g. intra-quarterly additions and removals) are released to the public. These can be found on the ASX 200 announcements page.

 

Where can you source these announcements?
All rebalance announcements below are published by the ASX in the announcement feed of any ASX company involved in a rebalance. They can also be obtained directly from the company.
